# Image quality: resolution, upscaling and layers (2026-11-01)

## Decision

**Generate at final 1920×1080 or higher whenever the image model actually supports it**, with additional overscan for camera moves; verify the returned pixel dimensions, focus and texture rather than trusting a prompt. If the native generator tops out below delivery size, preserve the original and use **Lanczos resize plus restrained, depth-masked unsharp** as the dependable CPU baseline. Upscaling cannot recover unknown real detail. Use a model-based candidate only after a same-frame comparison for geometry, temporal stability, halos and false detail; GAN-based super-resolution can hallucinate texture [3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCVW_2018/papers/11133/Gondal_The_Unreasonable_Effectiveness_of_Texture_Transfer_for_Single_Image_Super-resolution_ECCVW_2018_paper.pdf).

For the older hotel plates the actual source is **1376×768**, not 1080p. These tests use the committed originals from `reel/v09-cinematic`, with hashes in `image-metrics.json`, and create three matched before/after 1920×1080 pairs in `ab-reel/`. The pairs are experiments, not a new production film. Selected after stills were visually inspected; no continuous motion test or human quality panel was performed.

## Real-ESRGAN availability and honest fallback

The official Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan Linux binary v0.2.5.0 **installed**, release ZIP SHA-256 `e5aa6eb131234b87c0c51f82b89390f5e3e642b7b70f2b9bbe95b6a285a40c96` [1](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/releases). The only Vulkan device exposed here was **llvmpipe software CPU**, with no `/dev/dri`. A 96×96 → 384×384 `realesrgan-x4plus` probe with `-t 64 -j 1:1:1` exceeded a 90-second timeout and produced no result (`realesrgan-probe.txt`, exit 124). Thus Real-ESRGAN was **not operational within this host's test budget**, not “uninstallable” and not measured against Lanczos. Retry on a real Vulkan/CUDA GPU before rejecting it. Its paper describes the practical degradation model and limitations, including distorted lines and GAN artifacts [3](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2107.10833).

## Reproducible three-pair experiment

Run from the repository root with Python 3, Pillow, NumPy and OpenCV:

```sh
python3 research/imaging_experiment.py
```

On the same host, all six JPEGs and the CSV/JSON metrics regenerated byte-for-byte; other library versions may encode or round differently.

1. `ImageOps.fit` centre-crops the input aspect ratio minimally and resizes with **Lanczos** to 1920×1080. These are the `*-before.jpg` images. Both before/after are JPEG quality 95, 4:4:4; metrics below are computed from the **decoded delivered JPEGs at identical resolution**, not from different-sized sources.
2. The after image uses a thresholded unsharp mask: Gaussian σ **0.8 px**, gain **0.42**, ignore channel detail below **1/255**, progressively reducing sharpening in the far-depth mask. It is an edge-contrast adjustment, not newly resolved structure.
3. A hand-drawn, feathered far-plane mask crossfades the image toward Gaussian σ **1.6 px**, at most 48% blend. Thus blur increases continuously with the mask, rather than globally. This is only an approximation, not a true calibrated depth map or lens PSF. Real lens-defocus tools use depth/masks and can show edge bleed when depth boundaries are mishandled [3](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/2.91/compositing/types/filter/defocus.html); maintain subject/occlusion masks and review edges frame-by-frame for real work.
4. A maximum **10%** far-plane cool haze blend is applied in linear light. This approximates the common transmission-plus-airlight model `I = J·t + A·(1−t)` [3](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2020/papers/w51/Zhang_Color-Constrained_Dehazing_Model_CVPRW_2020_paper.pdf); it is not a measured physical atmosphere. Night scenes have multiple local light sources, so a single global airlight is an approximation [4](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/NTIRE/papers/Ancuti_NT-HAZE_A_Benchmark_Dataset_for_Realistic_Night-time_Image_Dehazing_CVPRW_2026_paper.pdf).
5. Two deterministic rain sheets—800 shorter/far marks and 160 longer/near marks, with different blur/opacity—are limited to the camera-facing exterior/window mask; the exterior shot permits rain across the lens plane. These are **static stills**, not proof of animated rain. In video, advect the sheets independently at different velocities, wrap without jumps, lock indoor masks to moving camera coordinates, and keep an occlusion/subject mask between planes.

## Measurements on the committed stills

Values below use fixed manually selected foreground/background ROIs listed in `image-metrics.json`. **Laplacian variance** is the variance of the 8-bit luminance Laplacian. **Acutance proxy** is the mean absolute normal luminance derivative divided by the four-pixel local edge contrast, at fixed strong baseline edge positions, in inverse pixels; contrast has a 10-level floor. This is **not** ISO 12233 slanted-edge SFR/MTF acutance. **Contrast** is luminance standard deviation in 8-bit levels. A proper calibrated acutance/MTF test requires an appropriate slanted-edge target and SFR method [1](https://www.imatest.com/imaging/sharpness/).

The selected background already had lower contrast than foreground in **all three before images**; the after treatment increases the separation in these ROIs. Because the regions contain different objects, their raw contrast is not an independent, universal depth score. Unsharp, depth blur, haze and rain change together, so this is a composite intervention, not an isolated causal test. Laplacian variance is sensitive to content, noise and sharpening and is meaningful here only as a same-shot proxy; a higher value can result from noise or halos rather than real detail [3](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/video-quality/articles-vqm/blur-and-detail-loss). `image-metrics.csv` includes background acutance and edge-gradient values as well.

## Avoid the “AI plastic” failure mode

- Do not smooth the whole frame or raise clarity globally; keep grain, material-specific microtexture and unevenness at believable scale. An 8-bit JPEG source cannot reconstruct lost texture by sharpening.
- Protect edges around lamps, glass and foreground cut-outs; stop when the bright/dark halo is visible at 100%. Make a no-sharpen control. If using a generative upscaler, inspect faces, lettering, keys, ledger geometry, repeated objects and window lines for invented features, not only sharpness numbers [3](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2107.10833).
- Derive masks from real depth/segmentation where possible; hand-feathered masks can smear across a lamp or doorway. Render temporal grain and rain as stable moving layers, not newly randomized full-frame noise; test a frame sequence and shots in motion before calling it cinematic.
- Compare native-size crops, the Lanczos control and each candidate at **matched output dimensions**. Add a calibrated slanted-edge chart/real high-resolution ground truth where available, and a blinded human visual test; the present metrics are diagnostic, not proof of perceived quality.
