## Speech methodology (do not substitute QA claims)
Actual narration FLAC/WAV decoded to 48kHz stereo for comparison with actual delivered AAC, with sample alignment estimated by crosscorrelation over the first 10s (±480 samples). Both available stems match at **0 sample lag**. Per-1s least-squares voice gain was fitted against mix; residual includes bed/effects AND codec/nonlinear/fit errors. Its ratio is conservative evidence for voice dominance, NOT separately isolated music LUFS, nor proof of each spoken word's semantic identity. Correlation/speech-energy coverage throughout the body plus committed word timing/caption comparison and sampled delivered captions bind the source to the cut numerically. Stem silencedetect logs independently establish source gaps. No listening, ASR or new synthetic source was used. This is the requested numeric verification, with its limits explicit.
