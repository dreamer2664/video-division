## 6. Blunt Self-Review

### What Works Well
- **Colour temperature as narrative engine:** The progression from dying `5600 K` dusk through `2100 K` sodium solitude and `4200 K` storm tension into `6500 K` silver dawn—returning in Shot 10 to the wet basalt harbour cobblestones from Shot 01—gives the 60 seconds genuine visual and musical closure.
- **Clean headless colourist pipeline:** Sub-pixel `4320×2430` Lanczos `zoompan`, `33×33×33` `.cube` LUTs + RGB `curves` + `gbrp` optical `blend` layers + Shot 07's SIMD `maskedmerge` live grade wipe run reliably in `10m 36s` under `800 MiB` RAM.

### What Is Weak (Honest Critique)
1. **2D affine motion vs true 3D parallax:** Even with `2.25×` Lanczos supersampling and moving rain/mist/water-shimmer `blend` layers, `zoompan` is a 2D planar transform—foreground iron bollards (Shot 06), gantry cranes (Shot 07), and the cast-iron streetlamp (Shot 10) do not parallax against background harbour lights the way live-action dolly footage or depth-mapped 2.5D projection would.
2. **Minor top-left badge overlap in Shot 10:** In Shot 10 (`46.4–50.5 s`), the top finial spike of the unlit streetlamp touches the lower edge of the translucent `6500 K · SILVER DAWN` stamp box; text remains `>17:1` legible, though framing the lamp slightly further right would keep the finial clear.
3. **Procedural piano timbre & compact loudness range (`LRA = 3.7 LU`):** While the coastal foley, foghorn fifth, and bowed pads blend convincingly in stereo reverb, the additive felt-piano notes in `build_audio.py` lack the organic hammer-felt micro-transients of a recorded acoustic piano, and continuous surf/pad underscoring keeps dynamic range relatively tight (`3.7 LU`).
