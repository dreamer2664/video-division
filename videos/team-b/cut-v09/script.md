# FOUR SECONDS AHEAD — cut v09

**Format:** 60.000-second, 30 fps, 9:16 procedural vector horror short, with 16:9 landscape derivative. No source footage, reference frames, or reference audio. All camera panels, rooms, clocks, monitor graphics, score, and sound are generated by the committed project.

## Premise
At 02:13, a night guard watches four security feeds. Every feed carries a millisecond timecode exactly 4.000 seconds ahead of the control-room wall clock. On CAM 04, he sees his own hand open before his real hand moves. He treats the monitor as a record; it is a preview. At the end, a fifth camera appears in the system though the building has only four.
