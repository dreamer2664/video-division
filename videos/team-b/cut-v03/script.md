# Cut v03 — The Quiet Signal

**Premise:** A small orange pulse crosses an abstract midnight city; the edit turns a lone signal into a shared pattern, then lets it resolve into calm. The subject is the act of noticing. All imagery and sound are original procedural creations; no stock, no narration.

## Production note
The shot timings are the render timeline contract. Motion, animated grid/window patterns, title cards, and sound are generated in the project; the cuts and changing visual density carry the narrative rather than static title slides.
