## Inherited observations, applied to a new film
- Across the three prior reference archives, 2,505 / 1,800 / 1,409 frames were decoded; the earlier study visually inspected labelled originals and contact sheets, with machine measurements on all 5,714 frames. It did not claim that a human watched every frame or listened to audio.
- Dark edges and restrained, centrally framed visual ideas held up better than detailed scenery; the measured reference means, file-by-file, were 28.7 / 48.3 / 38.8 luminance on a 0–255 scale. A single screen or doorway can carry the shot.
- A few highlighted words, outlined lower-centre captions and an anchored time marker established the design. Reference beats sampled at 0.20 s were often 2–4 words held around 0.6–1.8 s. Exact observations and caveats live in the prior study; none of the reference dialogue or soundtrack is reused.
- Earlier audio analysis was of mixed-reference band-energy proxies, not isolated stems or human listening. For this original production a new low drone, dry call bells and printer/relay textures are synthesized independently; a separate narration stem is mixed above them.
- The independently approved v07 film's discipline of convincing screen furniture, exit-counting and shift-board detail informs the *format*. WARD TEN's hospital arithmetic, script, layouts, sound and hook are new.

## v08 design and cadence
The board reads **10 beds**, the observations **10 patients**, the call-bell log **11**; the unadmitted Bed 11 appears in the handover. The later discharge record names the viewer as the patient. UI is original condensed type on deep navy/teal with yellow status and red exception states, and a fixed 02:17 A.M. header. A one-point-perspective receding hospital corridor and doorway view of an empty bed provide actual spatial depth amid the interface shots.

There are 30 planned cuts at 30 fps. Repeat **54, 60, 66 frames** ten times: each cycle lasts 1.8 + 2.0 + 2.2 = 6.0 s, for exactly 1,800 frames / 60.000 s and **2.0 s mean**, faster than v07's 2.4 s. Every line of the narration maps to the scripted cut range. Per-shot zoom and a restrained scanning cursor maintain movement between cuts.

Captions follow the committed voice's word boundaries, in **44 short cues of 2–5 words**. Each cue emphasizes its first word in yellow; the rest remains white with a dark outline. The timed pause after the printer line remains uncaptioned. The landscape derivative crops a central 1080×1080 story region from the 1080×1920 master, recreates the time marker in an original left sidebar, and reburns the same caption timings; it does not shrink the whole vertical composition or duplicate portrait captions.

The above are design intentions, not substitutes for the actual final-file scan, still inspections, or the explicit no-human-listening limitation in QA.
