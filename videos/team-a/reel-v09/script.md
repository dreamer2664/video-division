# PERFECT TIME — original v09 script and picture plan

**Premise:** At an after-hours call centre, the queue stays at one, every caller record is marked finished, and the line remains live. The dashboard's perfect forty-second average is not measuring completed calls: it measures callers being finished. An agent becomes the next voice on the line. This is fiction, not a depiction of an actual workplace or service.

## Narration

At two forty-one a.m., the call centre has one caller waiting.  
The queue never reaches zero.  
Each resolved call is replaced at once.  
The dashboard says average handling time: exactly forty seconds.  
Perfect. The phones say otherwise.  
Every line is still connected.  
You open the first caller's record.  
Status: finished. But the call is still live.  
You check the next record. Finished. Still live.  
The waveform keeps moving after the voice goes quiet.  
A timer appears beside your headset.  
The average was never measuring calls.

*[A deliberate pause while the counter and the connected line remain visible.]*

It was measuring callers.  
The system is not ending conversations.  
It is finishing the people on the line.  
Your timer reaches forty.  
The queue stays at one.  
A new agent picks up your line.  
Do not give them your name.
