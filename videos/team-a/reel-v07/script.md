# ARRIVAL LOG — original v07 screenplay and shot list

**Format:** 60.000 s, 30 fps, vertical first; a landscape derivative retains the whole portrait frame. **Premise:** in a twelve-floor office tower, a night guard logs the number of people exiting a lift that arrives at a nonexistent thirteenth floor. The monitor always shows the arriving car empty. The count increases; the guard discovers the booth has been travelling inside the lift. **Audience promise:** one coherent rule, three escalating violations, a reveal, and a loop into the next shift. The floor number is the final spoken sentence.

## Narration

At two forty-one in the morning, the night lift stops at floor thirteen.  
Your tower has only twelve floors.  
You're the guard. The lift protocol is simple.  
Every time the doors open, count the people who get out.  
Write the number in your log. Never count the people going in.

One steps out. The arrival camera shows an empty car.  
You write one.  
At the next stop, two step out. The car is empty again.  
You write two.  
Then three. Your log fills in your handwriting.  
But your pen is still on the desk.  
You check the building plan. There is no guard booth.

*[A deliberate, extended pause before the betrayal.]*

The camera turns. Your desk is inside the lift.  
You weren't watching it. It was carrying you.  
Four people step out.  
The next shift has already begun.  
The display reads:  
Floor thirteen.
