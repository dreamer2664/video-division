# script.md — v06 "IT ANSWERED"

pixel · Team A · branch `pixel/v06` · vertical master 1080×1920, 30 fps, 60.0 s.
Written **before** any frame is rendered. Narration is the spine; captions are burned in and match
the narration word for word (they are not paraphrase, so a muted viewer and a deaf viewer get the
same story). Spoken by a synthetic voice — see `QA.md` §Voice decision.

---

## The premise

A smart speaker in a dark flat answers a question nobody asked. It says your name, at 2:14 AM.
Then it starts replying to things you only *thought*.

The counted element is deliberately the smallest number in the film: **one**. One speaker, in one
flat, one person. The rule is about the assistant. The twist is about who is actually listening.
The last line is the phrase it should never have learned.

**Audience and promise:** someone watching a phone at 1 a.m., sound on, who wants 60 seconds of
escalation and one idea that lands cold. The promise is arithmetic rather than ghosts: nothing in
this film is supernatural, and everything it says is on screen to be checked.

**The idea I am defending:** a device that only ever heard *your* voice has exactly one person's
worth of language to learn from. That is why it knows what you only thought. It did not read your
mind. It read your language — and you were the only source.

when I'm alone."** The film never prints the phrase itself. That omission is the whole point: the
viewer supplies it, which is worse.

## Numbers, and how each one is drawn honestly

- **The counted element is 1.** On screen it is drawn as a 10,000-dot field with exactly one dot
  lit — the same fabric as v01, inverted. The other 9,999 dots are the same measure of "not one".
  Verified in code: `NDOTS = 10000`, `lit = 1`.
- **The clock is 2:14 AM** and never changes. It is not a countdown; the film's own clock is the
  timecode bar at the bottom, which is the only thing that moves continuously.
- **The count is stated, then re-stated, and never contradicted** — that is the deliberate
  inversion of the reference grammar (they show 4, count 3). Here the arithmetic is honest and the
  horror is that **one is enough**.

## Sound design (synthesised, no samples)

| layer | detail |
|---|---|
| drone | Am pedal, 55 Hz + 82.5 Hz saw pair through a slow low-pass sweep, −26 dBFS bed |
| sub hits | 41 Hz sine thumps on the four escalation cuts (8.0, 24.4, 34.0, 49.2 s) |
| room tone | filtered noise at −44 dBFS, present the whole film so no frame is silent |
| speaker SFX | the ring light's "listening" chime: 2.4 s of two-glass interval (1244/1661 Hz), dry, repeated at 8.0 and 34.0 s |
| count clicks | one dry click per dot event in beat 5 (1 + 3 clicks) |
| tape hiss | very low, under everything, so the mix never has a digital floor |

All generated in numpy at 48 kHz; mixed 8–12 dB under the narration; mastered to −16 LUFS /
≤ −1 dBTP. No reference audio is used — theirs is unlicensed to us.
