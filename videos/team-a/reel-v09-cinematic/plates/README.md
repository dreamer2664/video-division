# Photographic plate provenance

Ten distinct original AI-generated hotel photographs, 1376×768 at generation, were made after `reference-study.md`, the pre-render wall-frame decision, and the published `house-look-addendum.md` were committed. The renderer center-crops/upscales them with LANCZOS to 1920×1080 before grading; the committed JPEGs are its offline inputs. They are generated stills, not live-action footage or copied grade/reference frames.

Each generation prompt specified a single 16:9 35mm night photograph, wet architectural materials, three spatial depth planes, a warm brass practical against cool rain light, zero people, and no text/UI. The ten requested views were (1) rainy exterior awning, (2) lobby footprints, (3) ledger and key rack, (4) closed unlet-room corridor, (5) telephone close-up, (6) empty lift, (7) architectural lobby wall frame, (8) reception and wet trace, (9) telephone and ledger from another angle, (10) empty reception landing.

All ten were visually inspected. To reduce unintended extra lights, selected hot spots in 01/02/04/06/09 received local photographic exposure attenuation. Shot 03 had invented repeated key numbers; those numbers were inpainted and the rack softly defocused. The location, exact lamp design and object positions vary somewhat between independently generated views; continuity is not perfect, and the wet trace stopping at the desk is implied rather than animated. The dimmed ceiling fixture in shot 06 remains visible. These limitations should not be mistaken for a recorded live set. The image-generation limit for the turn was reached after these ten; no replacement plates were generated.

`manifest.json` records dimensions, treatments and SHA-256 hashes of the actual render inputs.
