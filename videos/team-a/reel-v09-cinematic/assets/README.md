# Production assets and attribution

- `../audio/narration_stem_48k.flac` and `../timing/word_timings.json` are the actual hotel-film voice and service word-boundary events. The fresh voice uses Edge `en-US-ChristopherNeural` at −12% and −4 Hz, with the published house close-mic EQ. Exact original MP3 streams, raw service events and SHA-256 provenance are in `../audio/tts_lines/` and `../timing/tts_source_manifest.json`. The offline renderer uses the committed FLAC, not the cloud.
- `Anton-Regular.ttf` is by Vernon Adams, distributed under the bundled `Anton-OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- The `.cube` LUTs and `house_motion.py` are first-party grade House Look assets explicitly authorized by the directive, copied from `look/` at `90394d8411185519fdc360f4c0ea26d0dc3f2822`; the local motion module has a configurable encoder CRF instead of a fixed 14. See `../house-look-addendum.md` for the source link. The renderer uses trilinear interpolation.
- The ten original generated photographic plates are hotel-film assets, not grade/reference frames. A small number of distracting unintended lights and garbled rack labels were retouched locally after inspection; all remain AI-generated photographs. Sound bed and Foley are newly synthesized with no grade/reference audio.
