- `narration.flac` and `word_timings.json` are the actual hotel-film narration and service word-boundary events, freshly generated with Edge `en-US-ChristopherNeural` at −12% and −4 Hz. The renderer uses these committed files offline.
- `Anton-Regular.ttf` is by Vernon Adams and distributed under the bundled `Anton-OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- `../luts/amber_ledger.cube` is grade's first-party `grade-v05` 33³ house LUT, explicitly authorized as a shared technical asset in `DIRECTIVE-2026-11-01-house-look.md`; its embedded creator credit is retained. The production applies this LUT with trilinear interpolation.
- Original generated photo plates, to be added after the published house-look has been reviewed, are hotel-film assets, not grade/reference frames. The sound bed and Foley will be newly synthesized; no grade/reference audio is used.
