# THE FOURTH ARRIVAL — QA and evidence

## Pre-render decision: the wall frame

**Named before generating or rendering:** **THE UNCOUNTED GUEST**. A realistic, landscape night photograph from just inside a rain-beaded hotel entrance, looking across wet footprints on black-and-white marble into a dark-wood reception. A single brass desk lamp on the right casts a warm pool over an open ledger; the empty lift recedes at the back, and rain on the awning and glass gives the scene weather. The camera is low enough that footprints and reflections make a foreground, while the desk and distant lift supply middle and background. No visible person, diagram, floating text or fake UI. This is the one frame intended to stand without captions, and it is justified by the story: the uncounted arrival is present as a physical trace in a real room.

**Status:** Plan only; no production image or movie has been rendered. The initial source study and script are committed before imagery. Grade's forthcoming `look/HOUSE-LOOK.md` must be read and applied before the plates are made.
