## Application, not plot copying

This is a rain-struck hotel night reception: dark wood, marble, a single brass desk lamp, a ledger, room keys, wake-up calls to a room never let, an uncalled lift and footprints. No locks, marks, bolted plate, keeper's booth or reference-format plot. The physical mismatch between two missing keys and three then four ledger arrivals is staged in a room, not visualized as a UI. The suspense comes from the number becoming correct only when the unseen arrival is counted.

Compose original **photographic generated** plates with foreground, subject, background and motivated light; no drawn geometry, slide, vector icon or legible fake interface. A warm practical anchors cool rainy shadows. Two moving rain depths belong outside the hotel window or on the awning, not as rain falling implausibly onto indoor furniture. Each frame should read as a real place at 2 a.m. Use 33³ trilinear grading, restrained lamp bloom, slow push/drift and a 1920×1080 30 fps landscape master. Word-boundary driven 2–5-word captions sit in the lower third. Voice, rain, the elevator, handset and musical sub-rumble remain separate production elements.

The pre-render wall frame and planned shot sequence are named in `QA.md` and `script.md`. The exact final edit will be reconciled to the committed narration telemetry before delivery. Do not infer human listening or continuous viewing from numeric verification.
