## 1. What if I never slept again?

- **Ticking quantity:** hours awake (counter, hours)
- **Rate of change:** 1 hour of wakefulness per real hour (film accelerated: 1 film second = 1 real hour). Underlying physiology is real-time.
- **Governing maths:** linear accumulation of wake time; impairment is a step function of hours awake (BAC-equivalence, microsleep onset, hallucination threshold).
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 0–16 h: baseline, sharp
  - 17–24 h: judgment impaired ≈ BAC 0.08–0.10% (legally drunk)
  - 36 h: microsleeps begin (involuntary seconds-long lapses)
  - 48 h: hallucinations/perceptual distortions begin; immune markers decline
  - 72 h: psychosis-level symptoms, paranoia, executive-function collapse
  - 264 h: verified world record (Randy Gardner, 1964)
- **Counter values (named frames):** 0 h at start; 24 h ≈ "legally drunk"; 48 h ≈ microsleeps; 72 h ≈ psychosis; film ends at 75 h (75 s at 1 h/s).
- **Closing joke:** "Seventy-five hours awake and I still haven't cleaned my room. That's not a what-if. That's a documentary with a liability waiver."
- **Source:** Cleveland Clinic / NIOSH (24 h awake ≈ 0.10% BAC); sleep-deprivation stage timelines (24/48/72 h); Randy Gardner 264 h record, 1964.

## 2. What if I never brushed my teeth again?

- **Ticking quantity:** bacteria on the teeth (counter, billions)
- **Rate of change:** plaque biofilm matures in 1–2 weeks undisturbed; ~10^11 bacteria per mm³ (≈1 mg) of plaque; saliva 10^8–10^9 bacteria/mL.
- **Governing maths:** biofilm growth — initial adhesion (hours), colony formation (days), mature complex biofilm (1–2 weeks), calcification to tartar (24–72 h mineralization).
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 4–12 h: pellicle colonized, early plaque
  - 24–72 h: plaque calcifies into tartar
  - 1–2 weeks: mature biofilm, gingivitis
  - 3–4 weeks: bleeding gums, halitosis
  - ongoing: periodontitis, decay
- **Counter values:** start ≈ 0 (just brushed); day 1 ≈ early colonies (10^7–10^8/cm²); day 7 ≈ mature plaque (10^10/cm²); day 14 ≈ gingivitis threshold.
- **Closing joke:** "Your mouth isn't a mouth anymore. It's a biofilm with opinions."
- **Source:** *Dental biofilm infections — an update* (Wiley, Apollonia 2017): saliva 10^8–10^9 bacteria/mL; mature biofilm 10^8–10^9 per mg; gingivitis in 1–2 weeks.

## 3. What if I drank nothing but coffee?

- **Ticking quantity:** caffeine in the body (counter, mg)
- **Rate of change:** +95 mg per cup (one 8 oz cup every 4 h); elimination half-life ≈ 5 h (exponential decay, k = ln2/5 ≈ 0.1386 h⁻¹).
- **Governing maths:** repeated-dose accumulation toward steady state: C_ss = dose / (1 − e^(−k·τ)) with τ = 4 h dosing interval → C_ss ≈ 95 / (1 − e^(−0.554)) ≈ 95 / 0.425 ≈ 223 mg average; peak after each dose ≈ dose + trough.
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 1 cup: alert (~95 mg)
  - 6 h: ~47 mg left + next cup → stack builds
  - 24 h: near steady-state, ~200 mg+ circulating
  - 48 h: jitter, anxiety, tachycardia
  - 72 h: insomnia, tremors, sleep deprivation compounding
- **Counter values:** dose 1 = 95 mg; trough before dose 2 ≈ 47 mg; peak after dose 2 ≈ 142 mg; steady-state trough ≈ 95·e^(−0.554)/(1−e^(−0.554)) ≈ 55 mg, peak ≈ 150 mg (oscillates ~55–150 mg around mean ~223 mg accumulated load — model: total body load settles near dose/k ≈ 95/0.1386 ≈ 686 mg for continuous approximation; using discrete, ~223 mg average over interval).
- **Closing joke:** "I'm not awake. I'm a caffeine molecule with a mortgage."
- **Source:** caffeine half-life ≈ 5 h in healthy adults; peak 30–60 min; CYP1A2 metabolism (multiple clinical references, e.g. health.com / lifestyle summaries citing ~5 h half-life, 10–12 h clearance).

## 4. What if I never blinked again?

- **Ticking quantity:** blinks skipped (counter, blinks)
- **Rate of change:** 15–20 blinks/min at rest → ~1,200/h → ~28,000/waking day → ~4.2M/year.
- **Governing maths:** linear blink accumulation; tear film breakup within seconds of non-blinking; tear film thins and destabilises without blink redistribution.
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - seconds: tear film breaks down, dry/gritty
  - 1 min: blurred vision, strain
  - minutes–hours: dry spots, corneal irritation
  - days: corneal ulcers, infection risk, vision impairment
- **Counter values:** start 0; 1 min ≈ 17 blinks skipped; 1 h ≈ ~1,000 skipped; 1 day ≈ ~28,000 skipped; 1 year ≈ ~4.2M skipped.
- **Closing joke:** "I haven't blinked since breakfast. My eyes aren't dry — they're filed under 'abandoned.'"
- **Source:** normal blink rate 15–20/min (≈28,000/day, ≈4.2M/year); tear-film breakup within seconds of non-blinking; NEI >16M Americans with dry eye.

## 5. What if I never cut my hair again?

- **Ticking quantity:** hair length (counter, cm)
- **Rate of change:** scalp hair ≈ 1.1 cm/month (≈0.37 mm/day; ≈13 cm/year); anagen phase 2–7 years; ~85–90% of follicles in anagen.
- **Governing maths:** linear growth L(t) = r·t, r ≈ 1.1 cm/month; terminal length set by anagen duration × rate.
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 1 month: ≈1.1 cm (needs a trim)
  - 6 months: ≈6.6 cm (collar length)
  - 1 year: ≈13 cm (shoulder length)
  - 3 years: ≈39 cm (mid-back)
  - 7 years (anagen limit): ≈92 cm terminal, then shedding
- **Counter values:** start 0 cm; month 1 ≈ 1 cm; year 1 ≈ 13 cm; year 3 ≈ 39 cm.
- **Closing joke:** "Seven years later I have hair and a ponytail and no idea where I left the scissors."
- **Source:** American Academy of Dermatology / Dyson hair science: ≈0.5 in (1.25 cm) per month, ≈6 in (15 cm) per year; forensic mean 1.12 cm/month.

## 6. What if I never cleaned my room again?

- **Ticking quantity:** settled dust (counter, mg per m²)
- **Rate of change:** house dust forms ≈ 6 mg/m²/day (German Environmental Survey); person sheds ≈1.5 g dead skin/day (~30,000–40,000 cells/hour).
- **Governing maths:** linear deposition D(t) = 6·t mg/m² (t in days); skin shedding is the dominant organic fraction.
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 1 day: ≈6 mg/m² (visible film)
  - 1 week: ≈42 mg/m² (dust bunnies form)
  - 1 month: ≈180 mg/m² (surfaces coated)
  - 6 months: ≈1.1 g/m² (mite habitat established)
- **Counter values:** day 1 ≈ 6 mg/m²; day 7 ≈ 42 mg/m²; day 30 ≈ 180 mg/m².
- **Closing joke:** "That's not a what-if. That's a documentary." (the reference's own test premise — included as the control, not our film)
- **Source:** German Environmental Survey (≈6 mg/m²/day house dust); Ohio State 2021 (≈6 mg/m²/day, ~830 mg/day in 1,500 sq ft); AAD (30,000–40,000 skin cells/hour).

## 8. What if I invested $1 every day?

- **Ticking quantity:** account balance (counter, USD)
- **Rate of change:** $1/day contribution; compound growth at market return r (≈7% annual, ≈0.0189% daily).
- **Governing maths:** future value of recurring deposits FV = P·[((1+r)^n − 1)/r], P = $1/day, daily rate ≈ (1.07)^(1/365) − 1 ≈ 1.855e-4.
- **Escalation ladder:**
  - 1 year: ≈$365 + interest ≈ $369
  - 5 years: ≈$2,100
  - 10 years: ≈$5,400
  - 30 years: ≈$37,000 (compounding dominates)
- **Counter values:** day 1 = $1.00; day 365 ≈ $369; year 5 ≈ $2,100.
- **Closing joke:** "Thirty years later I have enough for a used car and the warm glow of compound interest."
- **Source:** compound-interest future-value formula (standard finance); ≈7% long-term real equity return assumption stated as an assumption.

## 11. CHOSEN PREMISE — What if I never slept again?

**Chosen for the first film.** Quantity: **hours awake**, counter ticking continuously, film accelerated 1 film second = 1 real hour.

**Why this premise (defended):**
1. **One number that ticks naturally.** Hours awake is a single, intuitively understood quantity that climbs 1:1 with film time — no unit conversion, no abstraction. The counter *is* the story.
2. **Monotonic escalation is medically real.** 24 h ≈ legally drunk → 36 h microsleeps → 48 h hallucinations → 72 h psychosis. Every beat is strictly worse; the ladder is pre-written by sleep medicine, not invented.
3. **Real science, citable.** BAC equivalence (Cleveland Clinic/NIOSH), stage timelines, and the 264 h Gardner record are all verifiable. The comments can check the physics.
4. **Fits the 60–90 s target.** At 1 h/s, a 75 s film reaches 75 h — past psychosis into record territory — with a clean final number for the joke.
5. **Strong closing joke.** The final counter value (75 h) lands the joke directly; no new idea needed.
6. **Visually simple.** A climbing number + presenter describing each stage + sidekick reactions. Cheap to render, strong on mute.
